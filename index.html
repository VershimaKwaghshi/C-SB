C&SB Capital and Social Bond

A trader should not have to stop because money came first.

C&SB is where a trader begins with what is in hand, gets a real account to trade, and keeps going even after the account falls by half.

Why C&SB should exist

A trader finds a chance in the market. The account it needs costs $10,000. The trader has $1,000. The skill is there and the chance is real. Only the money is missing.

So the trader waits and saves, or buys a practice account where the money is never real. C&SB gives a real account from the first payment. The trader pays for it in small payments over 90 days.

Then a second problem arrives. Every trader has a bad stretch. When an account falls by half, the trader is out. One bad stretch ends a good trader.

C&SB is built for both problems. The account comes before the full price. When the account falls by half, people who choose to take part put money in so the trader keeps trading. The manager works to win it back.

Nobody in the story is left alone. The trader has a manager. The manager has a manager. Bonders stand beside the trader. Partners put in the money. Each one earns for taking part. That is why C&SB should exist. The rest of this page shows how it works and how to build it.

The whole path

This is the path a trader can follow on C&SB, from the first payment to the next chance. Everyone enters through a referral and buys an account. There is no direct deposit route.

  1. The trader joins through a referral, is verified and subscribes.

  2. The trader picks an account and pays for it in full or in small payments.

  3. The account opens and a manager trades it.

  4. Up to four sub accounts of the same size follow.

  5. The account falls by half.

  6. Social Bond puts money in and trading continues.

  7. Manager earnings win the money back and everyone who put in is settled.

  8. The money that comes back is used again for the next trader.

  9. Trading goes on.

Who takes part

The trader

Wants to trade a real account. Pays for it in full up front, or in installments over 90 days. The price is the account plus a 1 percent service charge. On installments, no profit can be withdrawn until the last payment is made.

The manager

Trades accounts for traders and has a trading record. Earns through seven channels. Also has another manager trading their own account, so every manager stands on both sides. A manager who reaches the 50 percent limit is isolated, and all seven channels go to recovery.

The Bonders

Ten join each Social Bond. Each has trading capital above the amount asked for and agrees to a hold of 10 percent of the amount on their own trading account. Each earns 0.8 percent of the amount. The holds are second in line. Nothing leaves a Bonder account unless the bond in the trader account is not enough on day 54.

The partner

A bank, a fintech, a microfinance bank, a cooperative or a trader who chooses to take part. Puts in the full amount and earns 8 percent of it within 30 days. If C&SB cannot collect from a Bonder account at that moment, the partner waits until it is fixed or recovery is made.

The referrer

Brings a trader in and earns a share while the referral stays active. If the referrer goes quiet, the trader can move to a new referrer.

The broker

Holds the accounts and keeps the rules, including the stop when an account reaches half. Pays C&SB a rebate of up to $12 on each lot traded.

C&SB

Passes the messages between everyone, sets the rules, keeps the records, and earns 6 percent of the amount on each Social Bond along with its other earnings. C&SB does not put in money for Social Bond.

Accounts and managers

Every account has a manager and every account has a known origin. The trader does not trade the main account. A manager does.

Five accounts

Each trader has one main account. After the account is funded, up to four sub accounts of the same size can follow. Five accounts of $1,000 make $5,000 of accounts to manage. It is not cash. Sub accounts can also be placed with other traders to manage.

Where accounts come from

An extra account can come from C&SB, from other traders, from banks and fintechs, from partners who supply accounts, from subaccounts, or from approved money partners. Each account carries a record of where it came from, who first owned it, who manages it, its size, when it was funded and everything that happened on it, including falls, Social Bond, recovery, profit, withdrawals and fees.

Choosing a manager

The Manager Marketplace shows the trader three managers. It picks them by trading record, results, risk, account size, how many accounts each manager already runs, trading style, past falls and who is free. A manager stays with the account until the trader changes manager or the platform makes a change. Every manager the account has had is kept on record. A manager can use an alias so a personal identity is not shown. The verified identity stays on file inside C&SB.

Becoming a manager

A trader becomes a manager with at least one month of live trading under their own name, or by passing a one month challenge. Manager is a role on the platform. It is not a licence. Every manager also has their own main account traded by another manager, chosen from the pool of eligible managers.

Submanagers

A manager can hand an account to a submanager. Without a submanager, profit splits half to the account owner and half to the manager side. With a submanager, the owner still gets half. The other half splits, a quarter to the original manager and a quarter to the submanager. Longer chains work the same way, following the people actually in the chain.

Trading Leaders

A Trading Leader coordinates a large group of up to 1,000 managers, 500 who trade in the morning and 500 in the evening. If each manager runs four sub accounts, that is up to 4,000 account positions. The leader coordinates. Each account keeps its own owner and its own manager history. The leader can set a rule, for example that two managers under the same leader do not open opposite trades on the same pair, so managers do not work against each other.

Seven ways a manager earns

Every manager earns through seven channels. C&SB tracks each one on its own.

  1. The main account

    The manager earns 50 percent of the profit on the main account assigned to them.

  2. Sub account 1

    The manager earns a 25 percent share of the fees from the first sub account.

  3. Sub account 2

    The same 25 percent share from the second sub account.

  4. Sub account 3

    The same 25 percent share from the third sub account.

  5. Sub account 4

    The same 25 percent share from the fourth sub account.

  6. Referrals

    The manager earns a 15 percent share of all revenue from the traders they refer directly.

  7. Social Bond services

    The manager earns the yield from putting money in or standing as a Bonder on Social Bonds, using their secondary accounts.

Referrals

Nobody joins on their own. Every trader comes in through a referral. C&SB records who referred whom, when, whether the trader qualified and became a buyer, what the referrer earned, whether it was paid, and the whole chain of referrals. Referral earnings are kept apart from the money in trading accounts. A manager earns a 15 percent share of all revenue from the traders they refer directly.

If a referrer goes quiet for 45 days, the trader can be moved to a new referrer through the Referral Marketplace. The trader keeps up to 5 points of the referral share and the rest goes to the new referrer. The first referrer, the current referrer and the full history are all kept. Nothing is erased.

How people earn

One person can earn in several ways at the same time. Each way has its own record, and none of them is mixed with the balance of an account.

The trader

Earns from the profit on their own account, from the extra accounts, from managing accounts for other traders, from submanaging, from referrals and from the Referral Marketplace.

The manager

Earns through the seven channels above. When a manager reaches the 50 percent limit, every one of these earnings goes to the trader recovery record until the account is rebuilt.

The partner

Earns 8 percent of the amount put in, paid at activation, within 30 days.

The Bonder

Earns 0.8 percent of the amount on every Social Bond joined. The ten Bonders together earn 8 percent.

C&SB

Earns from subscriptions, a 1 percent service charge on every account bought, 6 percent of the amount on each Social Bond, a 15 percent fee on withdrawals, a rebate from the broker on the lots traded and other services on the platform.

Why each side takes part

The trader

A fall does not end the story. The trader adds no money to rebuild the account, because the earnings of the manager do that. The trader also earns from referrals.

The broker

Traders who fall by half keep trading, so fewer of them leave and more lots are traded. The broker pays C&SB a rebate of up to $12 on each lot.

The partner

Puts money in for 30 days and earns 8 percent of the amount. The bond in the trader account stands behind it first, and the ten Bonder holds stand behind that.

The Bonder

Earns 0.8 percent of the amount for standing behind a Social Bond. Nothing is taken from a Bonder unless the bond in the trader account is not enough.

Social Bond in fifteen steps

The match in step 5 and step 6 looks at the trader, the amount, the account size, what is left in the account, who is free, the limit of each partner, what is already out, how fast each one answers, the past settlement record and how much money is on hand.

  1. The watcher sees the account fall by half. The manager is flagged as breached and isolated, and all seven channels go to recovery.

  2. C&SB checks that the trader is eligible.

  3. A Social Bond case is opened.

  4. The request is written for the amount that fell.

  5. A partner is matched. One partner can put in all or part, and what is not covered goes to the next.

  6. The ten Bonders are matched, each with a hold of 10 percent.

  7. The full amount is put in and the account is back to its full size.

  8. The trader receives the money and is told.

  9. The 22 percent activation fee is paid. It is a separate record from the money put in.

  10. The fee is shared at once, 8 percent to the partner, 8 percent to the Bonders and 6 percent to C&SB.

  11. The Social Bond is active and trading continues.

  12. The intercepted earnings go to the recovery record. If a second manager also breaches, both are isolated and their earnings are combined.

  13. The money out goes down as recovery comes in.

  14. The case settles. If money is still out, the bond in the account pays it first, then the ten holds equally.

  15. The money is released and used again.

Building C&SB from the ground up

Build the desks in this order. Each one stands on the one before it.

  1. The join desk

    Takes the referral, checks who the trader is, and starts the subscription. Keeps the subscription status, the renewal date, the payment history, failed payments, suspension and reactivation. Subscription money is never mixed with account money.

  2. The account desk

    Shows the trader the price, the payment plan, the service fee and the account rules before the first payment. Takes the payment, opens the account, counts the days and locks the funded amount until it is paid. Keeps the account size, the price, the fee, what is paid, what is left, every payment date, the start date, the end date, whether the account is issued, whether it is complete and whether it ended in a refund. Never calls an account real unless it is.

  3. The manager desk

    Lists managers with their trading records and shows a trader three choices. Records who trades which account, who has handed an account to a submanager, and every manager an account has had. Every account has a manager and every manager has a manager.

  4. The referral desk

    Records who referred whom and every step from the referral to the sale and the payment. Moves a trader to a new referrer when the old one is quiet for 45 days, and keeps the whole history.

  5. The broker link

    The broker holds the accounts and enforces the rules, including the stop at half. C&SB reads every account through this link.

  6. The watcher

    Reads every account all day. When an account falls by half, it opens Social Bond on its own.

  7. The Social Bond desk

    Opens each case, sends the request to a partner and to the ten Bonders, starts the 5 minute clocks. Takes the 22 percent fee and shares it at once, 8, 8 and 6 percent of the amount. Records the trader, the partner, the Bonders, the amount put in, the fee, the bond, the recovery, what is still out and whether the case is settled. The money put in is never C&SB revenue.

  8. The Bonder hold desk

    Places the 10 percent hold on each of the ten Bonder accounts and pays each Bonder the 0.8 percent. Releases the holds when recovery finishes in time. On day 54 it takes the bond in the trader account first and the holds only for what the bond does not cover, split equally.

  9. The intercept desk

    Watches every manager. When a manager reaches the 50 percent limit, flags the manager as breached, isolates them, and sends 100 percent of their earnings from all seven channels to the trader recovery record. If a second manager breaches, isolates both and combines their earnings. The trader never has to add money.

  10. The recovery desk

    Keeps the recovery record for each Social Bond. Recovered money pays down what is out. On day 54 it settles what is left from the bond in the account, then from the ten holds. Later earnings rebuild the account to its full size, or go back to the Bonders.

  11. The limits desk

    Watches how much money is out, in total and for each partner, Bonder, manager, trader, account, group of traders, country, market and case size. Stops a request that would go past a limit.

  12. The money record

    Every dollar has a line saying where it came from and where it went. Trader payments, account money, partner money and C&SB earnings never share a line. The same recovered dollar is never counted twice.

  13. The automation

    Runs the routine work. It works out payments, tracks each group of traders, checks who can be funded, works out funding milestones, assigns account sizes, watches manager room, spots falls, opens cases, sends requests, starts the timers, works out fees and shares them, tracks recovery, Bonder holds and partner money, works out withdrawal fees, referral shares and management shares, releases settled money, updates account history and keeps the record.

  14. The dashboards

    Each person sees their own. The next section shows what is on each.

Before launch, test the whole thing with recovery at 0, 25, 50, 75 and 100 percent, and with more Social Bonds opening than money coming back for a long stretch. The company section above does this.

What C&SB keeps on record

Every money event

A line with a number, the trader, the account, where it came from, where it went, the amount, the currency, the kind of event, the percentage used, the fee, what was left after the fee, the time, the manager, the submanager, the Social Bond case, the group the trader joined in, the referral, the status, and a link to what it undoes if it is reversed.

Every trader

Identity, referral, subscription, payment plan, group joined in, payments, target, funding, main account, extra accounts, where each account came from, who owns it, manager, submanager, Trading Leader, trading history, results, falls, Social Bond case, partner, Bonders, amount put in, fee, bond, recovery, withdrawals, revenue, money out, settlement and the history of each account.

Groups of traders

New traders join all the time, so each group that joins together has its own record. It keeps the entry date, the target, the payments so far, the fees, when funding is allowed, the funding status and the finish date. C&SB tells apart payments planned, money gathered, service fees, money given to accounts, funding waiting, money ready to use, money locked in trading, money released and finished plans.

The kinds of record the system needs

Users, referrals, Referral Marketplace listings, subscriptions, payment plans, payments, groups of traders, trading accounts, account sources, account owners, manager profiles, manager assignments, submanager assignments, Trading Leaders, trading results, falls, Social Bond cases, partners, Bonders, Bonder holds, money put in, recovery events, bonds, withdrawals, fees, revenue, money partners, money pools, money lines, reservations, notices and records of who did what.

The states money moves through

Planned, gathered, ready for an account, given to an account, trading, in a Social Bond, in recovery, held as a bond, released, ready to use again, settled and withdrawn.

What is not promised

C&SB does not promise a trading profit, that losses will be won back, that a trader will finish paying, that an account will be profitable, that a manager will be profitable, that a withdrawal will go through outside the rules, or that an account continues after a missed payment. All trading follows the account rules, the risk limits and the broker rules.
