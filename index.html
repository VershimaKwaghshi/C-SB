C&SB Capital and Social Bond

A trader should not have to stop because money came first.

C&SB is where a trader begins with what is in hand, gets a real account to trade, and keeps going even after the account falls by half.

Why C&SB should exist

A trader finds a chance in the market. The account it needs costs $10,000. The trader has $1,000. The skill is there and the chance is real. Only the money is missing.

So the trader waits and saves, or buys a practice account where the money is never real. C&SB gives a real account from the first payment. The trader pays for it in small payments over 90 days.

Then a second problem arrives. Every trader has a bad stretch. When an account falls by half, the trader is out. One bad stretch ends a good trader.

C&SB is built for both problems. The account comes before the full price. When the account falls by half, people who choose to take part put money in so the trader keeps trading. The manager works to win it back.

Nobody in the story is left alone. The trader has a manager. The manager has a manager. Bonders stand beside the trader. Partners put in the money. Each one earns for taking part. That is why C&SB should exist. The rest of this page shows how it works and how to build it.

Who takes part

The trader

Wants to trade a real account and does not have the full amount. Pays for the account in small payments over 90 days.

The manager

Trades accounts for traders and has a trading record. Also has another manager trading their own account, so every manager stands on both sides.

The Bonders

Up to 10 join each Social Bond. Each puts in up to 10 percent of the amount. Together they share 40 percent of the fee.

The partner

A bank, a fintech, a microfinance bank, a cooperative or a trader who chooses to take part. Puts in whatever the Bonders do not, and receives 40 percent of the fee.

The broker

Holds the accounts and keeps the rules, including the stop when an account reaches half.

C&SB

Passes the messages between everyone, sets the rules, keeps the records, and earns 20 percent of the fee along with its other earnings. C&SB does not put in money for Social Bond.

Building C&SB from the ground up

Build the desks in this order. Each one stands on the one before it.

  1. The account desk

    Takes the first payment, opens the account, keeps every payment, and counts the days. Everything else stands on this.

  2. The manager desk

    Lists managers with their trading records, shows a trader three choices, and records who trades which account. Every account has a manager and every manager has a manager.

  3. The broker link

    The broker holds the accounts and enforces the rules, including the stop at half. C&SB reads every account through this link.

  4. The watcher

    Reads every account all day. When an account falls by half, it opens Social Bond on its own.

  5. The Social Bond desk

    Sends the request to Bonders and partners, starts the 5 minute clock, matches who puts in what, takes the 20 percent fee and shares it 40, 40 and 20 at once.

  6. The recovery desk

    Collects the manager earnings from the six places and pays them back in order, bond first, then Bonders, then the partner.

  7. The money record

    Every dollar has a line saying where it came from and where it went. Trader payments, account money, partner money and C&SB earnings never share a line.

  8. The dashboards

    The trader, the manager, the partner and the Bonder each see their own. C&SB sees all of it.
