C&SB

A trader should not have to stop because capital came first.

A trader sees an opportunity and knows the account needed to trade it is $10,000. The problem is simple. The trader does not have $10,000 ready today. C&SB starts from that reality. The trader begins with what is available, moves toward the account, and keeps moving after the account becomes available.
